Privacy Policy: HMS Corporate Suite mobile app

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what the HMS Corporate Suite mobile app ("the app", package com.hms.manager) does with information. The app is published by High Minded Solutions ("HMS", "we"). Contact: sherif@hmsinfo.com · hmsinfo.com.

1. What the app is

The app is a business tool. It connects to the HMS ERP server of your employer or company (your "company"). You can only use it with an account your company created for you. We do not offer accounts to the public.

2. Who is responsible for your information

  • Your company decides why and how the business information in the app is used and is responsible for it (for example your name, attendance, approvals, documents and expense claims). The information is stored on your company's HMS server, or on the server your company chose for HMS to run for it.
  • HMS provides the software and handles that information only on your company's instructions.
  • For questions about your information, or to ask to see, correct or delete it, contact your company's administrator first. You can also write to us at the address above and we will pass your request to your company.

The app does not use advertising, does not sell information, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not read your contacts, messages, call history, microphone or health data.

4. Who receives information

  • Your company's HMS server (the destination of everything above).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers push notifications (it receives the push token and the notification text).
  • Google Play, which delivers and updates the app.
  • We do not share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. Storage on the phone and security

  • All traffic between the app and the server uses an encrypted (https) connection.
  • The app keeps a temporary copy of some screens on your phone so that it can work with a weak connection. This copy is removed when you sign out, when your company blocks the device, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Your company can block a lost phone from its server.

6. How long information is kept

On your company's server, for as long as your company keeps its records or the law requires. On your phone, until you sign out or uninstall the app. Location points sent while on duty are deleted from the server after the retention period your company has set.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You may refuse the location, camera or notification permission in your phone's settings. The features that need it (clock-in position, scanning, receipt photos, push messages) will not work, and the rest of the app still does.
  • You may switch off "Share my location while on duty" at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or export your information, and to object to its use. Contact your company's administrator, or write to us and we will forward your request.

8. Children

The app is for adult employees. It is not meant for people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will publish the new version at this address and change the date above. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
